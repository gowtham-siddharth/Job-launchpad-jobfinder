Privacy Policy

LinkedIn Job Auto-Opener  ·  Chrome Extension

Overview

LinkedIn Job Auto-Opener ("the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that automates walking LinkedIn job search results and opening matching company apply pages in background tabs. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By signing in and using the extension, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Account data

When you sign in, we collect your email address through Firebase Authentication (Google). This is used solely to verify your identity and check whether your account has been approved to use the extension.

Usage data

Each time you start a job search run, the extension records a usage event in Firebase Firestore. This includes:

  • Your user ID (derived from your Firebase account)
  • The date of the run
  • A count of how many job searches you initiated that day

This data is used exclusively to enforce per-user daily usage limits set by the administrator. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Local data (on your device only)

The following information is stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and never leaves your device:

  • Your filter settings and named profiles
  • The run state (opened / scanned / skipped counts)
  • A history of LinkedIn job IDs you have previously opened (to avoid duplicates)

Information We Do Not Collect

The extension does not collect, transmit, or store:
  • Your browsing history outside of LinkedIn job pages
  • The content of job descriptions or any LinkedIn page content
  • Your LinkedIn credentials or any LinkedIn account data
  • Financial, payment, or health information
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Any data from websites other than linkedin.com

How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication – to verify you are a registered and approved user before any extension feature activates.
  • Access control – to allow an administrator to approve, deny, or set daily limits for individual accounts.
  • Usage limits – to enforce any daily job-search limits associated with your account.

Your data is never used for advertising, analytics sold to third parties, credit assessment, or any purpose unrelated to operating the extension.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with any third party. The only external service used is Firebase (Google LLC), which acts as the authentication and database backend. Firebase processes data under Google's privacy terms. All communication with Firebase occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections.

For more information on how Firebase handles data, see firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Data Retention

Account and usage records in Firebase are retained for as long as your account is active. Local data stored in your browser (settings, job history) is retained until you clear it via the extension's "Clear history" button or uninstall the extension.

Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Access – a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Deletion – removal of your account and associated usage records from Firebase

To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page will change. Continued use of the extension after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
